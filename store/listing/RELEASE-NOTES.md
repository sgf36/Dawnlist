# ReleaseNotes — en-GB source, for 1.6.1

Release notes are read by people deciding whether to update, and by the
reviewer deciding whether to pass. Everything here is user-facing; nothing
names an internal limit, a build variant change or a server-side detail.

British English, no Oxford commas, no abbreviations.

---

After each search, Dawnlist now tells you how many postings it read, how many searches it ran and how long it took, so a quick run no longer leaves you wondering whether anything was missed.

When a run comes back with no postings at all, the summary now says so plainly and points you to your searches and location. When one of your job sources could not be reached, it says that too.

---

## What this deliberately does not say

- **No mention of diagnostics.** The opt-in activity log is a support tool,
  switched on by a file, and nobody reading release notes would use it.
- **No mention of job-source changes.** The LinkedIn Job Library was removed
  before any customer relied on it; the managed feed is unchanged for them.
- **No mention of how a run is timed or counted.** The buyer sees the
  summary line; the stage timings behind it are internal.
