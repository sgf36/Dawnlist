## The short version

Dawnlist runs on your computer. Your CVs, your fit brief, your background
factsheet, your tracker and every draft it writes stay on your machine.

Dawnlist uses **your own** Anthropic API key to read job postings and draft
messages. That conversation is between you and Anthropic, on your account.
Those documents are never sent to Dawnlist's own servers, and Dawnlist never
holds a copy of them.

The only thing that reaches Dawnlist's servers is the **job search itself** —
the search terms and filters you save — plus a count of how much you have used,
so a licence cannot be shared by a thousand people.

Dawnlist does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, and does not
build a profile of you for anyone else's benefit.

## What stays on your computer

Stored in your operating system's application-data folder, and never
transmitted to Dawnlist:

- the CVs and documents you add;
- your background factsheet and fit brief;
- every posting Dawnlist has assessed, and its verdict;
- your decisions — pursued, rejected, saved for later;
- your tracker: companies, stages, contacts, dates, and the evidence log;
- every draft message.

Your Anthropic API key and your licence key are held in your operating system's
credential manager (Windows Credential Manager, or the macOS Keychain), not in
a file Dawnlist writes.

You can export all of it, or delete all of it, from within the app. Deleting it
removes it from your machine; there is no copy elsewhere to ask us for.

## What goes to Anthropic, on your key

To assess a posting or draft a message, Dawnlist sends to Anthropic:

- the job posting being assessed;
- your fit brief and background factsheet, so the model can judge a stated
  requirement and keep a claim truthful;
- for a draft, the recipient's name and company.

**This uses the API key you supply, on your own Anthropic account.** Dawnlist is
not a party to it and holds no copy. Anthropic's handling of that data is
governed by your agreement with them and their own policies — worth reading, at
anthropic.com/legal. You can revoke the key at any time, in their console, and
Dawnlist will stop working rather than fall back to anything else.

## What goes to Dawnlist's servers

Three things:

**Job searches.** The search terms, job titles, locations and filters you save
are sent to Dawnlist's feed service, which queries job-data providers and
returns matching postings. These are searches, not documents: no CV, no
factsheet, no draft, and no assessment ever passes through this service.

**Identifiers of postings you have already been shown.** So the same posting is
not fetched twice. Identifiers only — never the posting, never its content, and
never anything you did with it.

**Licence and usage counts.** Your licence key, whether it is active, and a
daily count of how many postings were returned to it. Counts only — never the
postings themselves, and never their content. This is what stops one licence
being shared indefinitely.

Our servers log errors by type, never by content.

## How long things are kept

Split by purpose, because no single period is right for all of them:

- **Usage counts and posting identifiers** — while your licence is active and
  for twelve months afterwards, to answer support and billing questions.
- **Your record of accepting these terms** — for as long as your subscription
  runs and for six years afterwards. It is contractual evidence, and destroying
  it when a subscription ends would destroy it at exactly the point a
  disagreement becomes likely.
- **Everything on your own machine** — for as long as you keep it. You can
  export or delete it from inside the app, and there is no copy elsewhere to
  ask us for.

## Payment

Subscriptions are handled by **Paddle**, who are the merchant of record. Your
name, email address and payment details go to them; Spencer Fields Software
never sees or stores your card details. Paddle also records that you accepted
these terms at the point of purchase, and that record is mirrored against your
licence.

## Job postings themselves

Postings come from third-party job-data providers and are public listings. They
may contain the names of recruiters or hiring managers, which Dawnlist stores
locally alongside the posting so you can see who to contact. That information
is not shared onward.

## What Dawnlist never does

- It never sends an email, message or application on your behalf. Every
  outreach is a draft you open and send yourself, from your own mail app.
- It never reads your mailbox. Dawnlist has no access to your email account,
  and asks for no password or authorisation to one. Where you choose to add
  emails — job alerts, or your own sent messages so drafts match how you write
  — you supply the files yourself and they are processed on your machine.
- It never sells or rents personal data.
- It never uses your data for advertising, or to train any model. Anthropic's
  API does not train on API traffic; see their policy.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal
data we hold, object to processing, or request a copy in a portable format.
Most of this you can do yourself, immediately, from inside the app, because
almost everything is on your own machine.

For what our servers hold — your licence and usage counts — write to
**privacy@spencerfields.com**. We will respond within one month.

You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## International transfers

Dawnlist's feed service runs on Cloudflare's global network, so a search may be
processed outside the United Kingdom. Requests you make to Anthropic go to the
United States, under your own agreement with them. These transfers rely on the
UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the providers' own safeguards.

## Children

Dawnlist is a tool for people looking for work and is not directed at children.
We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
